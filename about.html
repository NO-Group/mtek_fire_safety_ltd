About M-Tek

Building a safer Nigeria, one facility at a time

Certified supplier and service provider of fire safety, security and solar solutions — headquartered in Kaduna and serving organisations nationwide since 2012.

Our Story

A decade of trusted safety partnerships

M-Tek Fire & Safety Ltd was incorporated on 5th December 2012 with registration number RC: 1082534. From our base in Kaduna, we have grown into one of Northern Nigeria's dependable suppliers of fire safety, security and solar equipment.

We help organisations and individuals protect lives and property through reliable, standard-compliant safety solutions — from extinguishers and alarm systems to security devices and complete solar installations.

Today, our team supports offices, schools, hospitals, factories, warehouses, fuel stations, places of worship and residential estates with tailored safety and power solutions, backed by professional installation, refilling, maintenance and training services.
