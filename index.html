Who We Are

Protecting lives and property since 2012

Incorporated on 5th December 2012 (RC: 1082534), M-Tek Fire & Safety Ltd provides fire safety, security and solar products and services across Nigeria.

Our team supports offices, schools, hospitals, factories, warehouses, fuel stations and residential estates with tailored safety and power solutions — combining quality products with professional installation and after-sales support.

  • Certified expertise and compliance with relevant standards
  • Professional installation and responsive after-sales support
  • Extinguisher refilling, pressure testing and routine inspection
