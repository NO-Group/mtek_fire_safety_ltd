In Depth

Two of our most-requested services

Fire Extinguisher Sales, Installation & Refilling

M-Tek provides certified fire extinguisher sales, professional installation, refilling and maintenance services across Nigeria. We supply CO₂, DCP, Foam and Water extinguishers suitable for residential, commercial and industrial environments.

Our technicians ensure correct extinguisher selection, strategic placement, pressure testing and compliance with safety regulations. We also offer routine inspection and refilling services to keep your equipment operational at all times.

Industries served include offices, schools, hospitals, factories, warehouses and fuel stations.

Fire Safety Training, Consultancy & Risk Assessment

M-Tek delivers professional fire safety training and consultancy for organisations and individuals. Our programmes improve emergency preparedness, regulatory compliance and workplace safety culture.

We provide fire risk assessments, evacuation planning, extinguisher handling training and safety audits tailored to your facility — working with management to identify hazards and implement effective prevention strategies.

Training suits corporate organisations, schools, hospitals, construction sites and industrial facilities.
