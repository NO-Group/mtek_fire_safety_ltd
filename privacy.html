4 Cookie & Client-Side Local Storage Policy

We believe in complete transparency regarding how our website stores data on your computer or mobile device. This section provides a detailed technical explanation of our client-side storage architecture.

4.1 Plain Static Architecture (No Invasive Ad-Tracking Cookies)

`www.mtekfiresafety.com` is built using clean HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript (`script.js`, `products.js`, `shop.js`). WE DO NOT UTILIZE INVASIVE THIRD-PARTY BEHAVIORAL ADVERTISING COOKIES, CROSS-SITE TRACKING PIXELS, OR PERSISTENT MARKETING SURVEILLANCE SCRIPTS.

4.2 Use of HTML5 Web Storage (`localStorage` — Key: "mtekCart")

In order to provide a seamless e-commerce shopping cart experience on our Shop page (`shop.html`), our custom JavaScript application (`shop.js`, lines 181 and 186) utilizes standard HTML5 Web Storage (`localStorage`).

  • How It Works: When you click "Add to Cart" on any fire extinguisher, CCTV kit, or solar inverter, `shop.js` executes the browser command localStorage.setItem("mtekCart", JSON.stringify(cart)). When you reload the page or navigate to another section of our website, the script executes localStorage.getItem("mtekCart") to reload your selected items.
  • Why It Is Strictly Necessary: Storing the `"mtekCart"` object locally in your web browser allows you to browse our 700+ product catalogue without losing your cart selections, without requiring you to register a user account, and without sending your browsing habits to a remote database server.
  • Duration & User Control: Data stored in the `"mtekCart"` key remains on your device solely for your convenience until:
    1. You click the "Clear Cart" button in the shopping modal;
    2. You click "Order on WhatsApp" or "Order on Email", which automatically executes clearCart() upon compilation; or
    3. You clear your web browser's local cache or storage via your browser settings.

4.3 Third-Party Map Embeds & External Service Cookies

Certain pages on our website feature embedded interactive content provided by external platforms:

  • Google Maps Iframes: To help clients locate our Head Office (YY 12 Kazaure Road, Kaduna) and Branch Office (Sir Patrick Yakowa Way, Kamazou), we embed Google Maps interactive iframes (`maps.google.com`). When loaded, Google may set performance or geolocation cookies governed by Google’s independent Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We configure our map embeds with loading="lazy" and referrerpolicy="no-referrer-when-downgrade" to maximize user privacy and prevent unnecessary background loading.
  • External Messaging Platforms: Clicking our floating WhatsApp button (`wa.me/2348033498452`), Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok social links redirects your browser to external platforms operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. and ByteDance Ltd. Those third parties operate their own cookie and data processing frameworks over which M-Tek has no control.

9 Your Statutory Data Subject Rights (NDPA 2023)

The Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 confers upon you comprehensive, enforceable statutory rights regarding any personal data we hold about you. You may exercise these rights at any time without charge by submitting a request to our Data Protection Team.

  1. Right to be Informed (Section 34): You have the right to transparent, clear explanation of what data we collect, why we collect it, and who receives it (as provided in this Policy);
  2. Right of Access (Section 34): You have the right to request a formal copy of your personal data currently archived in our customer records;
  3. Right to Rectification (Section 35): You have the right to request immediate correction or updating of any inaccurate, outdated, or misspelled personal or corporate contact information;
  4. Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Section 35): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the original purpose, where you withdraw consent, or where processing is unlawful—subject to mandatory statutory retention exemptions such as Nigerian tax invoices and fire safety audit trails;
  5. Right to Restriction of Processing (Section 36): You have the right to demand that we suspend processing your data while an accuracy dispute or objection is being investigated;
  6. Right to Data Portability (Section 38): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used electronic format for transfer to another service provider where technically feasible;
  7. Right to Object to Processing (Section 36): You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or communications based on legitimate interests;
  8. Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 35): Where processing relies on your consent, you have the right to withdraw such consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to withdrawal;
  9. Right to Lodge a Complaint with the NDPC: If you believe that your data privacy rights have been infringed by M-Tek, you have the right to lodge a formal complaint with Nigeria's supervisory authority:
    • Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)
    • Headquarters: No. 5 Donau Street, Maitama, Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.
    • Website: www.ndpc.gov.ng

9.1 How to Exercise Your Statutory Rights

To exercise any of your statutory rights, please send a written request via email to mtekfiresafetyltd@gmail.com with the subject line "NDPA Data Subject Right Request - [Your Name]". Our Data Protection Officer will acknowledge your request within five (5) business days and provide a full formal response within thirty (30) calendar days in accordance with statutory guidelines.
